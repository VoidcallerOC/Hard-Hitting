Before You Go

Foxwoods FAQ

Is this the shop or the show?

Both. Hard Hittin' Cards is the Southington shop. Hard Hittin' Card Shows is the promoter. Foxwoods is the flagship event.

Where do I buy tickets or a table?

Treasure (ontreasure.com). Every dated show page on this site links straight to the live event.

Is there a trade night?

Yes — free Saturday trade night, typically 6–8 PM. Confirm the room on the event page before you go.

Do you take PSA at the show?

Yes. Hard Hittin' Cards accepts PSA submissions at the shop and at our booth on the show floor.
