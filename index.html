Before You Come In

Good Questions

Quick answers for collectors, sellers and first-time visitors.

Do you buy cards and whole collections?

Yes. Bring a single, a binder or a lifetime collection — sports or any TCG. We'll take a look and make a fair offer in cash or store credit, no appointment needed. Sitting on something big? Call ahead at 860-316-1075 so we can set aside the time.

Can I trade instead of selling?

Absolutely. Use the offer starter above to tell us what you have and what you're chasing, then bring it to the counter. We trade with the shop and the community to help you track down the exact card, set or player you're after.

Do I need an appointment for the counter?

Nope — walk-ins are welcome any time during shop hours. Appointments only help for very large collections so we can give you our full attention.

How do card-show tickets work?

All show tickets and full vendor details are handled on Treasure (ontreasure.com). Each show on our calendar links straight to its Treasure page — grab tickets there, and follow @hardhittincardshows for date drops.

How do I get a table at a Hard Hittin' show?

Reserve your table through Treasure from the "Reserve a Table" section above. Prime floor space at Foxwoods and beyond goes fast, so lock it in early — or DM @hardhittincardshows with questions.

What do you carry on the shelves?

Sports cards plus every major trading card game — Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh! and One Piece — from vintage rookies to the newest set drop, singles to sealed wax, plus all the supplies to protect the hits.
